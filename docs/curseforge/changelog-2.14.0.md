Wireless Automate 2.14.0 brings its **own guide book**: an open two-page book in graphite and brass, with tabs, search, recipes and 3D scenes you can rotate. The mod **no longer depends on GuideME**.

**The new guide book**

- An open book with two pages side by side, at a fixed size so the pixel art and the text stay sharp at any window size.
- **Tabs on the right edge**, one per group (Getting started, Tools, Storage, Energy and crystals, Panels, Integrations, Reference), each opening that group's index.
- **Search** on the title page: just start typing (or Ctrl + F). Accents and capitals don't matter; Enter opens the first result.
- **Recipes** come from the recipes actually loaded, with alternative ingredients cycling every second.
- **Navigation:** ◀ ▶, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel turn the pages; the mouse side button or Backspace goes back; the book reopens where you left it during the session.
- **Press G** over any item of the mod (inventory, chests or JEI) to open its page.
- A new item icon: the closed book, with the porcelain Eye pulsing on the cover.

**3D scenes**

- **Drag** inside a scene to rotate it, the **mouse wheel** zooms (0.5× to 3×) and a **double click** goes back to the page's angle.
- **Annotations** outline the highlighted blocks and draw lines between points; hover one to see its text.

**Other changes**

- The screen image commands are now in English, like the other commands: `/wa screen images`, `/wa screen image remove|ban|unban`.
- The Linker says "1 machine linked" in the singular.
- The Crystal Repairer's render does a little less work per frame.

**Compatibility note:** the network protocol is unchanged (**40**), so 2.13 servers and clients still work together. Worlds from 2.13 load as they are. NeoForge 21.1.248 or newer is still required. **GuideME is no longer needed** by this mod. Players who had the old guide book get the new one once, on their next login; in a pack that still has GuideME (other mods use it), the old book stays in the inventory without use and can be thrown away. All optional integrations remain optional.
