# Network Tablet

<ItemImage id="wirelessautomate:network_tablet" scale="2" float="left" />

An overview of all your networks, from anywhere. Open it with a right-click, or with the **Open
Network Tablet** key (unbound by default; set it in Controls) while the tablet is in your inventory.

<br clear="all" />

<RecipeFor id="wirelessautomate:network_tablet" />

- **List:** search, filter by role (extracts, inserts...) and **Select** to move many nodes between
  networks at once. Click a node to open its screen remotely.
- **Map:** top-down view colored by status; click a point to open the node.
- **Statistics:** throughput per type, mod time per tick, full destinations and unloaded chunks.
- **Networks:** create, color, members and privacy.
- **Groups:** bundle the networks of one system to pause and resume them all at once.
